How to Host a Murder Mystery Party: A Step-by-Step Guide for First-Time Hosts

A murder mystery party turns an ordinary dinner into a game where every guest plays a character, and one of them is secretly the killer. It sounds complicated, but with a good kit the host only needs to read a short script. Here is how to do it.

1. Choose the kit and the number of guests

Most kits are written for a fixed number of characters, often 6 to 8. Check that the game still works if someone cancels: good kits mark which characters are optional and let anyone (any gender, any age over 12) play any role.

2. Send invitations 2–3 weeks before

Each guest gets their character name, a one-line description and a costume idea. People enjoy the party much more when they have had a few days to think about their character.

3. Prepare on the day

  • Print the character booklets, evidence cards and name tags.
  • Put each guest's booklet in an envelope with their name on it.
  • Read the host script and the solution once, so you can answer questions.

4. How the evening runs

RoundWhat happens
ArrivalGuests meet in character; the host announces the murder
Round 1: SecretsEveryone reads their booklet and starts asking questions
Round 2: Where were you?Alibis are shared and the first evidence cards come out
Round 3: The evidenceThe last clues: photos, letters, phone logs
AccusationsEveryone writes down who they think did it and why
The revealThe host reads the solution and the timeline

Plan 2 to 3 hours with dinner. Serve the main course between rounds 1 and 2 and dessert before the reveal.

5. Tips from experienced hosts

  • Nobody needs to act. Guests can read lines straight from the booklet. Shy guests relax after the first round.
  • Prizes: best costume, best detective and best liar.
  • Theme the food and music: a Christmas manor, a 1920s speakeasy, a cruise ship.

Why it works so well at Christmas

Holiday dinners are when friends and family are already together for a whole evening. A murder mystery gives the evening a story, gets people who barely know each other laughing, and makes the night memorable.
