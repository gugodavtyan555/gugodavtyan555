How slot machines work: RTP, volatility and RNG

Slots are the most popular casino games. Here is how they work, so you can play with your eyes open.

Random number generator (RNG)

Every spin is decided by a random number generator. Each spin is independent of the previous ones: a slot does not “remember” losses and is never “due” a win. There are no winning schemes for slots.

RTP: return to player

RTP shows what share of all bets a slot pays back to players over a very long period. For example, an RTP of 96% means that, on average, $96 of every $100 bet is paid back as winnings, and $4 is the casino's edge. Over a short period the result can differ a lot either way.

Volatility

  • Low: wins are frequent but small.
  • Medium: a balance between frequency and size.
  • High: wins are rare but can be large. Your balance can go down for a long time.

Bonus features

Many slots have free spins, multipliers and bonus rounds. They are always described in the game's “Rules” or “Paytable” section, so check it before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Can you predict a win on a slot?

No. Every spin is decided by a random number generator and does not depend on the previous ones.

What does an RTP of 96% mean?

Over a very long period the slot pays back on average 96% of all bets. In the short term the result can differ a lot either way.

Which slots suit beginners?

Beginners usually find low or medium volatility slots easier: wins come more often, though they are smaller. You can try demo mode before playing for money.
