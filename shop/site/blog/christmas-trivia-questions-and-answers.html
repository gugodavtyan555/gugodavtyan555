Christmas Trivia Questions and Answers: 20 Easy and Fun Questions for Kids and Adults

Christmas trivia is the easiest party game to run: one host, a few pens and teams of three or four. Here are twenty questions that work for mixed ages, from easy to tricky, with the answers right after each one.

20 Christmas trivia questions

  1. In which country did the tradition of decorating Christmas trees begin? Answer: Germany
  2. What plant do people traditionally kiss under? Answer: Mistletoe
  3. What gift is given on the first day in 'The Twelve Days of Christmas'? Answer: A partridge in a pear tree
  4. How many gifts are given in total in 'The Twelve Days of Christmas'? Answer: 364
  5. What three gifts did the Wise Men bring? Answer: Gold, frankincense and myrrh
  6. Where does Santa Claus live? Answer: The North Pole
  7. In which country is fried chicken a popular Christmas meal? Answer: Japan
  8. Which red-leaved Christmas plant comes from Mexico? Answer: Poinsettia
  9. What is the date of Boxing Day? Answer: December 26
  10. Who wrote 'A Christmas Carol'? Answer: Charles Dickens
  11. What is the name of the grumpy miser in 'A Christmas Carol'? Answer: Ebenezer Scrooge
  12. How many Christmas ghosts visit Scrooge, not counting Marley? Answer: Three
  13. What is the name of Scrooge's clerk? Answer: Bob Cratchit
  14. Which British dessert is often set alight before serving? Answer: Christmas pudding
  15. Which striped sweet is shaped like a hook? Answer: A candy cane
  16. What do children traditionally leave out for Santa in the USA? Answer: Milk and cookies
  17. What are Santa's helpers called? Answer: Elves
  18. How many sides does a snowflake usually have? Answer: Six
  19. What is the name of the four weeks before Christmas? Answer: Advent
  20. In which country was the carol 'Silent Night' first performed? Answer: Austria

How to run a great trivia round

  • Play in teams. Teams of 3–4 mix ages and let shy guests help without being on the spot.
  • Read each question twice and give 30 seconds before the next one.
  • Swap answer sheets between teams for marking. It's faster and more fun.
  • Keep a tie-breaker: "How many lights are on our tree?" Closest guess wins.
  • Kids' version: let younger players answer out loud and give a point for every good try.
