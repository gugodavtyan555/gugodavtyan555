Responsible gambling: how to stay in control

Gambling is entertainment, not a way to earn money. Over the long run the casino always has a mathematical edge.

Simple rules

  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Decide in advance how much you will spend and do not go over it.
  • Do not try to win back losses.
  • Never gamble with borrowed money or money meant for important expenses.
  • Take breaks and keep track of time.

Casino tools

Account settings usually let you set limits on deposits, bets and time, and turn on self-exclusion for any period.

Warning signs

  • You play longer and spend more than you planned.
  • You hide your gambling from people close to you.
  • Gambling affects your work, studies, sleep or relationships.
  • You feel irritated when you cannot play.

If you recognise yourself, stop and seek help from a specialist. Free and anonymous support exists, for example Gamblers Anonymous groups.
