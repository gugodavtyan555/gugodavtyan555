15 Christmas Party Games for Adults (and Mixed Ages) That Actually Get People Playing

The best Christmas party games are simple to explain, work with a drink in one hand, and let quieter guests join in without performing. Here are fifteen that we have seen work, grouped by the kind of party you are hosting.

Easy icebreakers (first 30 minutes)

  1. Who Am I? Stick a card with a Christmas character on each guest's back. They ask yes/no questions until they guess it. Great for mixing people who don't know each other.
  2. Christmas Would You Rather. Read a question ("Would you rather wrap every present or cook the whole dinner?") and let people move to one side of the room. No skill needed, lots of talking.
  3. Two Truths and a Christmas Lie. Everyone shares three holiday memories; the others guess which one is invented.

Table games (after dinner)

  1. Christmas trivia. Play in teams of three or four. Forty questions take about 30 minutes. Mix easy ones ("What plant do people kiss under?") with a few hard ones so every team scores.
  2. Finish the Carol. Read the first line of a carol, teams write the missing word. Surprisingly competitive.
  3. Christmas word scramble. Hand out a sheet of scrambled festive words; first team to solve them all wins.
  4. Christmas bingo. Works for 6 to 30 people. Use different cards for every player so there is one winner at a time.

Gift games

  1. Left-Right gift exchange. Everyone holds a wrapped gift while you read a story. Every time you say LEFT or RIGHT, gifts move. Whoever holds a gift at the end keeps it. Takes 5 minutes and works for any age.
  2. White elephant / Yankee swap. Guests bring a wrapped gift under a set price and can "steal" opened ones. Set a limit of two steals per gift so it ends.
  3. Gift bingo. While someone opens presents, guests mark off items on their card ("socks", "candle", "something homemade").

Active games

  1. Christmas charades. Act out a movie, song or object. Split into two teams and use a one-minute timer.
  2. Scavenger hunt. Hide clues around the house, each one leading to the next. Rhyming clues make it feel special for kids and adults.
  3. Minute-to-win-it. Stack ornaments, move candy canes with a spoon, unwrap a present with oven gloves.

The big one: a murder mystery night

  1. Christmas murder mystery. Each guest plays a suspect with secrets and an alibi. Over three rounds the clues come out, and everyone votes on the killer. Plan 2–3 hours and 6–8 players. It turns an ordinary dinner into the party people talk about for years.
  2. Escape-the-room puzzle. A printable escape room with locks and codes works for teams of kids or adults at the same table.

Which games for which party?

PartyBest picks
Office partyTrivia in teams, bingo, Would You Rather
Family with kidsScavenger hunt, Left-Right game, charades, word scramble
Friends' dinnerMurder mystery, Finish the Carol, white elephant
Big group (15+)Bingo, Who Am I?, trivia with teams of five

Hosting tips

  • Print everything the day before and put each game in its own envelope.
  • Small prizes (chocolate, a candle, a silly hat) make people try harder.
  • Play the loud games before dinner and the table games after.
  • Always have a tie-breaker question ready.
